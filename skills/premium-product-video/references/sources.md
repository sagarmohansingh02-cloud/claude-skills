# Sources

This skill synthesizes two public posts with production experience from building launch films. Both posts are paraphrased and credited here, not copied.

- **leo (@leomeethewoo), founder of Intently**, "Make your product videos look expensive (Apple Framework)", X Article, 2026-09-25: https://x.com/leomeethewoo/status/2103529310208606701
  Contributed: brand intention and the 3-color constraint, design-before-motion principles (one shot = one idea, room to breathe, key object centered, background never competes), eased and overlapped motion, flowing transitions, adaptive rhythm, the BPM bands, subtractive sound design.

- **Rexan Wong (@rexan_wong)**, thread on making professional motion-graphic videos with Claude Opus, X, 2026-09-26: https://x.com/rexan_wong/status/2103707054108299437
  Contributed: the direction loop (reference → context dump → three storyboards → one still per scene → render → director notes), real UI components instead of invented UI, and camera-vocabulary revision notes.

- **Worked example:** the Cache 2.0 launch film (open-source macOS app, github.com/sagarmohansingh02-cloud/cache). It supplied the source-of-truth brand extraction from app code, springs ported to easing, and multi-format re-layout.

The "when the rules bend" section comes from applying these rules next to editorial podcast-trailer work, where hard cuts and linear wipes are deliberate.
